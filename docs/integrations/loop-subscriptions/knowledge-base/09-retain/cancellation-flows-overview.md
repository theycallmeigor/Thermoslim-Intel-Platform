# Cancellation flows overview

Multi-step journeys designed to reduce churn by offering alternatives and collecting feedback.

### Three Key Parts

1.  **Benefits Page:** Reminds customers of subscription value.
2.  **Cancellation Reasons:** Gathers insights and suggests alternative actions (skip, swap).
3.  **Cancellation Offers:** Last-step incentives (discounts, gifts).

### Preferences

*   **US ARL:** Compliant one-click cancellation for specific states.
*   **Button Behavior:** Allow direct cancellation or restrict it.
*   **Minimum Orders:** Message for early cancellation attempts.
*   **Prepaid:** Immediate cancellation or after the cycle.

### Customization

All headings, descriptions, and labels are customizable in the "Texts" tab.
