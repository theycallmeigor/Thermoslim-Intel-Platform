# Flows

Loop flows allow for automated actions based on specific triggers (When, If, Then).

### Logic

*   **When (Trigger):** New subscription, recurring order placed, products/discounts modified, or payment failed.
*   **If (Condition):** Subscription (plan, value, count), Product (title, collection), Customer (spent, tags), Bundle, or Demographics.
*   **Then (Action):** Update subscription (discounts, trial, mystery reward), update product (add/remove, swap), add/remove customer tags, or manage bundles.

### Reward Notifications

Inform customers via portal banners or emails (direct or via 3rd party like Klaviyo or Omnisend).
