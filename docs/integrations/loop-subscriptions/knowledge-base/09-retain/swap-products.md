# Swap products

Product swap allows customers to replace items in their subscription without cancelling.

### Enabling the Swap Option

1.  Navigate to **Loop > Retain > Product Swaps > Preferences**.
2.  Enable permission for the customer portal.
3.  Set discount settings for swapped products.
4.  Define **Available products** and **Excluded products**.

### Portal Experience

*   **Customer:** Clicks "Edit" next to the product, opens swap drawer, and selects a new item.
*   **Admin:** Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions**, search for customer, and click the pencil icon next to the product to change it.

Products can also be swapped in prepaid subscriptions.
