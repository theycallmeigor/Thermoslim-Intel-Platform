# Benefits page

The first step in the cancellation flow, acting as a "positive interruption" to remind customers of subscription perks.

### Setup

Navigate to **Loop admin > Retain > Cancellation flows > Benefits page > Create new page**.

### Customization

*   **Conditions:** Show specific pages based on product, order count, frequency.
*   **Variables:** Personalize with subscriber name or loyalty points.
*   **Media:** Image or video enhancement.
*   **AI:** Generate tailored messages.
