# Cancellation offers

Powerful incentives presented as a final attempt to retain customers who are about to cancel.

### Setting up Cancellation Offers

1.  Navigate to **Loop admin > Retain > Cancellation flows > Offers > Create new offer**.
2.  Configure conditions and choose incentive type (Add free gift or Add discount).
3.  Set **Offer applicability**:
    *   **Reasons:** Trigger only for specific cancellation reasons.
    *   **U.S. ARL:** Enable for direct offers in compliant states (CA, CO).
    *   **Cool-off period:** Eligibility wait time for future uses.
4.  Configure the **Offer details drawer** (description and button text).

### Combine with Skip or Delay

Use Flows to combine an offer with other treatments, e.g., "10% off + skip next order" using the "Is cancellation offer applied" condition.

### Customer Experience

Subscribers select a cancellation reason, and if criteria are met, an offer drawer appears for them to accept or proceed with cancellation.
