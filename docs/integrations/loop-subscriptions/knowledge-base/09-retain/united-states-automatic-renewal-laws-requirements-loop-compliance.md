# United States Automatic Renewal Laws: Requirements & Loop Compliance

Compliance requirements for US state and federal laws (FTC, California, New York, Colorado).

### Pre-purchase Disclosures

Terms (billing frequency, price, trial details, cancellation method) must be clear and conspicuous. Shopify handles this at checkout.

### Express Informed Consent

Customers must actively agree to terms (no pre-checked boxes).

### Online Cancellation (One-Step)

Online cancellation must be as easy as sign-up. Loop's "United States ARL compliance" setting (enabled for CA and CO) ensures a compliant flow.

### Compliant Flow Steps

1. Show Offers (incentives).
2. Fallback to Benefits page.
3. Cancellation Reasons.
4. Direct Cancellation (if no other steps enabled).

### Renewal Notices

Reminder emails are required for trials or long-term plans (e.g., 15-45 days for annual subs).
