# Mystery rewards

Surprise incentives where the exact reward isn't revealed until unlocked. Creates "aha!" moments to divert customers from negative actions (cancel/skip).

### Setup

1.  **Trigger:** E.g., new subscription created.
2.  **Condition:** E.g., order count is 2.
3.  **Action:** "Add mystery reward".
4.  **Claim Behavior:** "Claim-based" (requires login) or "Automatic".
5.  **Notification:** Configure reveal experience settings.

### Customer Experience

A reveal experience appears on the portal when the subscription qualifies. Mystery boxes are removed after the order.
