Learn how to integrate Glow with Loop to reward subscribers with loyalty points, enable discount redemptions, and boost trust through reviews and engagement.

**Glow** is the reviews and loyalty platform for e-commerce, helping you establish brand credibility by building trust and giving your customers a voice. By **integrating** Loop with Glow, you can reward loyalty points to your subscribers based on their order activity and let them redeem points by applying discount codes generated by Glow on their subscriptions.

### Key features

*   **Reward:** Giving your consumers a voice and attracting new ones are made easier by product reviews, ratings, and user-generated content. Utilizing clever integrations, you can highlight your reviews where it matters.
    
*   **Loyalty:** Building consumer trust and rewarding them for their interaction with you are the goals of point systems, VIP tiers, and referrals.
    

This article will walk you through Glow integration with Loop, detailing the setup process and its benefits.

Glow integration is available on the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# Integrations details

## How to integrate Loop with Glow

### Prerequisites

Before starting the integration process, we need to make sure these things are in place.

*   You have an active Glow account. If not, please create from [here](https://apps.shopify.com/smile-io)
    
*   Glow main app toggle is enabled.
    
*   Glow widget should be enabled on Shopify store - Live theme
    
*   Merchants should be on Loop’s Starter plan or above.
    

If the above prerequisites are met, follow these steps to complete the process.

1.  Navigate to **Loop > Tools & apps > Apps > Glow** and click on **Connect**.

##   
How can customers use the discount codes?

To add the Glow discounts on the upcoming order, we need to make sure these things are in place.

### Prerequisites

*   **“Apply discounts”** to be enabled in Loop > Customer portal > Preferences
    
*   The customer should be able to access the Loop subscriber portal.
    

Follow these steps to complete the process.

1.  Subscriber logs in to the customer portal.  
    ​
    
    [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/kmcpsev1/1813146763/d26abcb3b879e82ce1bee3bacf72/original?expires=1774894500&signature=d9ff0c693c940479c77d30a6d5abf41ba01ec5e5c07ff67a712fad9f9371259b&req=dSgmFch6m4ZZWvMW1HO4zVtuc0i%2BIhlUt90KWxT2hd%2BqerDQ0VOVPLZSKnhK%0AnJW0%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/kmcpsev1/1813146763/d26abcb3b879e82ce1bee3bacf72/original?expires=1774894500&signature=d9ff0c693c940479c77d30a6d5abf41ba01ec5e5c07ff67a712fad9f9371259b&req=dSgmFch6m4ZZWvMW1HO4zVtuc0i%2BIhlUt90KWxT2hd%2BqerDQ0VOVPLZSKnhK%0AnJW0%0A)
    
2.  Once the prerequisites are met, the customer can redeem the Glow discount code directly from the customer portal, and then they can apply the discount code directly to the discount section on their subscription portal.
