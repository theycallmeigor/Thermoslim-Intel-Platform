Explore how Loop integrates with leading loyalty apps to boost retention, rewards, and subscriber engagement.

**Loyalty** programs reward customers for continued engagement, helping brands strengthen retention and lifetime value. When paired with **subscriptions**, they turn recurring purchases into rewarding experiences that keep customers subscribed longer.

Loop integrates with leading loyalty partners: **Yotpo, Rivo, RiseAI, Okendo, Bubblehouse, Influence, Friendbuy and other apps** that let brands reward subscribers for renewals, referrals, and other key actions directly within their subscription journey.

* * *

# What is Loyalty?

Loyalty programs are structured systems that encourage customers to keep engaging with a brand over time. They work by rewarding repeat purchases and meaningful actions - like referrals, reviews, or social shares - through points, discounts, or exclusive perks.

A well-designed loyalty program builds emotional connection, increases brand trust, and turns one-time customers into long-term advocates.

# How Loyalty enhances subscriptions journey?

Combining loyalty with subscriptions turns routine renewals into rewarding experiences. By integrating with loyalty apps and subscription experience, brands can motivate customers to stay subscribed longer, explore new products, and engage more deeply with their brand.

Loyalty-driven rewards - such as points for renewals, using earned points for placing an upcoming subscription order, or exclusive discounts for long-term subscribers, help reinforce positive behavior while reducing churn.

Together, loyalty and subscriptions create a continuous growth loop - every renewal drives engagement, and every engagement strengthens retention.

The entire loyalty experience on Loop is powered by integrations with our trusted partners, ensuring seamless functionality and enhanced flexibility for merchants.

# Major use cases

Integrating loyalty app with Loop opens multiple opportunities to reward and retain customers effectively.

1.  **Loyalty widget in customer portal:** Display loyalty points and unused coupons directly in the Loop's customer portal to boost engagement and transparency.  
    ​
    
2.  **Seamless points redemption:**Allow customers to redeem points on active subscriptions; redeemed points auto-apply as discounts and re-credit if removed.  
    ​
    
3.  **Improve add product conversion using loyalty points:** Allow customers to use loyalty points when adding products through upsell sections, campaigns and quick actions, boosting add-on conversions.  
    ​
    
4.  **Loyalty variable support in notifications:** Display loyalty points and unused coupons in Loop notifications and the Customer Portal to improve engagement and upcoming order realization.  
    ​
    
5.  **Redeem points in cancellation flows:** Show a “Redeem Loyalty Points” action during cancellation to incentivize retention and reduce churn.

3.  **Use loyalty variables in notifications**  
    ​  
    ​**What it does:** This feature allows merchants to include loyalty points related information directly in their notifications.  
    ​  
    ​**How it helps:** By using loyalty data into notifications, it enhances customer engagement and keeps subscribers informed about their rewards status. Personalized reminders about points and available rewards motivate customers to take action, such as making a purchase or renewing their subscription.  
    ​  
    ​**Example:** A subscriber cancels their subscription but later decides to reactivate it. Upon reactivation, they receive a personalized email notification that shows their loyalty points balance. The email encourages them to use their points for a discount on their next renewal, making the reactivation process more rewarding and seamless.

5.  **Boost add-on product conversion with loyalty points**  
    ​  
    ​**What it does:** This feature enables customers to use loyalty points to pay for products in the upsell section and during add-product quick actions and campaigns.  
    ​  
    ​**How it helps:**  
    \- Increased visibility of discounts: Customers see the final price with loyalty points applied in the upsell banner, making discounts clear upfront.  
    \- Merchants can enable use loyalty points in the **add-product** quick action settings for events and campaigns.  
    \- Higher conversion rates: A lower final price encourages more add-on product purchases, boosting conversions.  
    ​  
    ​**Example:** A subscriber views an upsell offer for a new product in the customer portal. With the **"Allow customers to pay with loyalty points"** option enabled, they see the final price after applying their loyalty points, making the discount visible upfront. For instance, a $100 product price drops to $70 after using 200 loyalty points. The subscriber decides to add the product to their subscription, encouraged by the immediate discount.
