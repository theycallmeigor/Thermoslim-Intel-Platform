Learn how to integrate Rebuy with Loop to enable personalized upsells, cross-sells, and smart subscription conversions across your Shopify store.

**Rebuy** is a personalization platform for Shopify that helps merchants boost revenue and improve the shopping experience with smart upsells, cross-sells, and tailored customer journeys.  
​  
​**Learn more:** [Rebuy](https://www.rebuyengine.com/)

### Key features

*   **Smart cart:** Create a dynamic shopping cart that suggests relevant products, subscriptions, and bundles in real time.
    
*   **Personalized recommendations:** Show shoppers the right products at the right time with AI-driven recommendations.
    
*   **Omnichannel upsells:** Deliver upsell and cross-sell offers across your store, checkout, email, and SMS to maximize engagement.  
    ​
    

Rebuy integration is available on the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# Integration details

## How does Rebuy work with Loop?

Rebuy integrates seamlessly with Loop to enhance subscription-based offerings and optimize the customer experience. By combining Rebuy's personalized recommendations with Loop's subscription management features, businesses can unlock powerful use cases to drive more conversions and increase customer retention.

## Major use-cases & benefits

**Use case**

**Description**

**Benefit**

**Subscription pop-ups**

Rebuy can trigger pop-ups on multiple touchpoints (E.g. 'Add to cart button) offering discounts to convert one-time purchases into subscriptions, encouraging customers to sign up for recurring deliveries at a lower price.

Increases subscription conversions while providing an incentive for customers to commit to a recurring order.

**Smart cart upgrade offers**

Instead of presenting one-time purchase options, Rebuy displays "Subscribe & Save" offers directly in the shopping cart.

Increases subscription conversions while providing an incentive for customers to commit to a recurring order.

**Reorder landing page upsells**

When customers return to reorder past purchases, Rebuy can promote subscription options on the landing page.

Encourages customers to switch to a subscription for convenience and savings on repeat purchases.

**Post-purchase upsells**

After a customer completes a purchase, Rebuy can display relevant subscription or product upgrade offers without disrupting the checkout flow.

Boosts sales by presenting additional offers in a non-intrusive way, leading to higher average order value.

**Dynamic bundles**

Rebuy can create "Buy More, Save More" bundle offers, encouraging customers to purchase multiple items at a discount.

Increases the order value and promotes subscription sign-ups through bundled savings.

**Cross-sell**

Rebuy can recommend complementary products at checkout, such as suggesting a matching belt to a shirt.

Increases sales by showing relevant cross-sell items, while also offering subscription options for additional products.

By combining Rebuy's personalized recommendations with Loop's subscription capabilities, you can effectively grow your subscriber base and improve customer retention.

## How does the integration work?

Rebuy works hand-in-hand with Shopify to pull in the subscription selling plans you’ve set up through Loop. Since Shopify is the source of truth, Rebuy simply fetches those details so the right subscription options show up for your customers.

The benefit? Everything stays in sync automatically. You don’t have to juggle subscription data in different tools, and your customers always see the latest options without extra effort on your part. It’s designed to feel seamless for you as a merchant, while the behind-the-scenes syncing runs asynchronously to keep things fast and reliable.

## How to connect Loop with Rebuy?

Integrating Loop subscriptions with Rebuy is a straightforward process that enables your store to display personalized subscription offers. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started.

**Prerequisites:**

You must have a Loop subscription app installed on your store.

Follow these steps to complete the process:

1.  Log in to your Rebuy Admin panel and navigate to **Integrations** and then select **Subscriptions integration**. Toggle the integration **ON** and confirm. A “**Connected**” status will appear once successfully connected.

##   
Limitations

1.  **Selling plan availability:**Because Rebuy pulls selling plans straight from Shopify, it doesn’t apply Loop’s availability rules. This means selling plans set to be hidden in at different touchpoints such as storefront may still appear on smart carts and other Rebuy features.

2.  **Bundle selling plans:** Rebuy also doesn’t apply checks for bundle-specific selling plans. If you’ve created a bundle plan in Loop, it may still show up in Rebuy touch points like Smart Cart even if only the standalone product is added without the other bundle items.

# Advance use-cases

## Using Loop bundles with the Rebuy smart cart

If you’re using Loop bundles and want to hide the “**Switch to subscription**”, "**delete**", or "**Upgrade**" buttons only for bundle line items in Rebuy smart cart to restrict customers from getting bundle selling plan discount on individual items, reach out to [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bccfc9ccccd3cec8fcd0d3d3cccbd3ced792dfd3) or the Rebuy team to disable this option for you.
