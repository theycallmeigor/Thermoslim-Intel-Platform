Learn how to integrate Rise.ai with Loop to automate store credits, and reward loyal subscribers for improved retention.

**[Rise.ai](https://rise.ai/)** is a loyalty and store credit platform for Shopify that helps brands drive retention and repeat purchases through automated rewards and refunds.

### **Key features**

*   **Store credit automation** – Issue credit for returns, cancellations, and promotions to encourage future purchases.
    
*   **Gift card management** – Sell, send, and manage digital gift cards with ease.
    
*   **Loyalty rewards** – Reward customers for purchases, referrals, and milestones with store credit.  
    ​
    

**Rise.ai integration is available exclusively on the Loop Pro plan.**

* * *

# Integration details

## How does Rise.ai work with Loop

*   Customers can now seamlessly redeem Rise store credits on their Loop subscription orders, driving higher retention and customer satisfaction.
    
*   Store credits information are visible right within the Loop customer portal and can be applied effortlessly to upcoming subscription charges.
    

## Why use store credit over loyalty points?

Unlike loyalty points, store credit feels like real money. It’s easier to understand, more flexible to use, and has a higher redemption rate, making it a powerful tool to drive repeat purchases and reduce churn.

# FAQs

#### Does the Loyalty widget support multilingual text?

You can change the texts by heading over to Loop > Integrations > Influence > "Texts" tab.

#### How do I change the color of the widget?

The widget can be customized from customer portal > Themes > styles. You can even add a custom css to match your brand tonality!
