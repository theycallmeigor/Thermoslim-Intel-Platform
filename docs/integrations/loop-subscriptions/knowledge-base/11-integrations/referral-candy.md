Learn how to integrate Referral candy with Loop to run automated referral programs that drive new subscriber acquisition and boost customer retention.

Referral candy helps Shopify stores attract new customers, increase sales, and grow their brand through referral and affiliate programs.

### Key features

*   Set up a referral or affiliate program that incentivizes subscription purchases
    
*   Enable post-purchase popups & emails to enroll customers in your referral program
    
*   Automate customer rewards, you can choose from discount coupons or cash
    
*   Customize your referral program with your logo, images, and brand colors
    

Referral candy can serve as the best platform to run a customer referral program that works with your store's subscription program, acquiring new customers while retaining existing ones.

Referral candy integration is available on both the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# Integration details

## Why integrate with Referral candy?

By integrating with Referral candy, you can reward your subscribers based on their referrals and let them unlock their rewards by applying discount codes generated by Referral candy with their subscriptions.

*   Make discount codes generated by Referral Candy compatible with Loop Subscriptions.
    
*   Increase subscription retention by allowing customers to redeem their referral rewards while placing subscription orders.
    

## How to set up Referral candy to work with subscription purchases?

Let's say you want to offer an Advocate Reward of 20% off for subscription products only and a Friend Reward of 30% that can be used on either a subscription or a one-time purchase.

### Advocate reward

1.  Go to your Campaign, then go to Rewards  
    ​
    
2.  Select **Advocate reward**, then choose **Coupon reward**.  
    ​
    
3.  Switch the **Enable coupon for subscription apps** toggle on.  
    ​
    
4.  Select one of the options:
    
    1.  One-time purchase only
        
    2.  Subscription products only
        
    3.  Both one-time purchases and subscription products  
        ​
        
    
5.  Click **Save reward**.  
    ​

# FAQs

#### How many times can a discount code generated by Referral Candy be used?

The number of times a discount coupon can be used depends on the "Usage Limit" configured during the reward setup on Referral Candy. You can choose any value from 1 to 99 for the usage limit.

#### Will this coupon discount be applied over and above the selling plan discount?

Yes, the coupon discount will be applied over and above the selling plan discount. For example, if you set up a 10% selling plan discount for a product priced at $10, and a customer applies 20% discount code generated by Referral Candy, then the final price for the customer will be calculated as:  
​

Price after 10% selling plan discount = $10-$1 = $9

Final price after an additional 20% Referral Candy discount = $9 - $1.8 = **$7.2**
