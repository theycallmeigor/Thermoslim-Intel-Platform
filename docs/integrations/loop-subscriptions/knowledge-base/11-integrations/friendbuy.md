Learn how to integrate Friendbuy with Loop to launch referral and loyalty programs that boost customer acquisition, engagement, and retention.

Friendbuy is a referral and loyalty platform designed to help businesses boost customer acquisition and retention through customizable programs. It empowers brands to drive new referrals by leveraging existing customers, and simplifies campaign management with seamless integrations and automated reward distribution.

### Key features

*   **Referral program software:** Empowers customers to spread the word with best-in-class referral program software to drive new customer acquisition and accelerate growth.
    
*   **Loyalty program software:** Rewards and retains customers with enterprise-grade loyalty program software to effortlessly boost engagement and maximize lifetime value.
    
*   **Seamless integrations:** Offers deep integrations with leading platforms for e-commerce, subscription billing, email, SMS, and customer data platforms, facilitating streamlined workflows and personalized communications  
    ​
    

Friendbuy loyalty is available exclusively on the Loop Pro plan.

# Integration details

## How to integrate Loop with Friendbuy?

### Prerequisites

Before starting the integration process, we need to make sure these things are in place.

*   You should have an active Frienbuy account. If not, please create from [here](https://www.friendbuy.com/).
    
*   Your **Shopify account is connected** to your Friendbuy account.
    

If the above prerequisites are met, follow these steps to complete the process.

1.  Navigate to **Loop > Tools & apps > Apps > Friendbuy** and click on **Connect**.
