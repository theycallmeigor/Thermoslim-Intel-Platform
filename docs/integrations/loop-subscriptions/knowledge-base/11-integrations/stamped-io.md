Learn how to integrate Stamped with Loop to combine reviews and loyalty programs that build trust, reward engagement, and drive repeat purchases.

Stamped is the reviews and loyalty platform for e-commerce, helping you establish brand credibility by building trust and giving your customers a voice.

### Key features

*   **Reward**: Giving your consumers a voice and attracting new ones are made easier by product reviews, ratings, and user-generated material. Utilizing clever integrations, you can highlight your reviews where it matters.
    
*   **Loyalty**: Build consumer trust and reward them for their interaction with the brand is the goal of the referral app.
    

This article will walk you through stamped integration with Loop, with details of the setup process and its benefits.

Stamped.io integration is available on both the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# Integration details

## Why integrate Stamped.io with Loop?

By integrating Loop subscriptions with Stamped, you can reward loyalty points to your subscribers based on their order activity and let them redeem points by applying discount codes generated by stamped on their subscriptions.

*   Make discount codes generated by stamped compatible with Loop subscriptions.
    
*   Increase subscription retention by allowing customers to redeem their loyalty points while placing subscription orders.
    

## How do I set it up with Loop?

To integrate stamped with Loop the merchant needs to have satisfied the prerequisites before going through with the integration on the app

### Prerequisites

*   You have an active Stamped account. If not, please create it from [here](https://stamped.io/account/signup?utm_source=loopsubscription&utm_medium=app&utm_source=loop-subscriptions&utm_campaign=stamped).
    
*   Merchants should be on Loop’s Starter plan or above.
    

Follow the steps below if you have satisfied the prerequisites

1.  Go to Loop > Tools & apps > Loyalty and referral > Stamped.

## How can my customers use the discount codes?

To use the discount codes generated by the stamped rewards app. The Merchant needs to ensure the following prerequisites are met before their customers are able to apply the discounts on subscription contracts

### Prerequisites

*   “Apply discounts” to be Enabled in Loop > Customer Portal > Preferences
    
*   The customer should be able to access the Loop Customer portal
    

Once the prerequisites are met, the customer can redeem the stamped discount code directly from the customer portal, and then they can apply the discount code directly to the discount section on their subscription portal.
