Learn how to integrate UpPromote with Loop to run affiliate and referral programs that drive new subscriber acquisition and boost brand growth.

**UpPromote** is an all-in-one affiliate and referral marketing platform that enables brands to launch, manage, and scale partner programs. It helps drive new customer acquisition through affiliates, influencers, and referral links, boosting revenue and brand reach.

### Key features

*   **Affiliate & referral campaigns:** Create custom programs with flexible commission structures and branded registration pages.
    
*   **Real-time tracking:** Track clicks, orders, and conversions via affiliate links, coupons, or emails.
    
*   **Automated payouts:** Manage payments easily with PayPal integration and bulk payout options.
    
*   **Seamless Shopify integration:** Quick setup with native Shopify sync and customizable affiliate portal.  
    ​
    

UpPromote integration is available on both the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# How to integrate Loop with UpPromote?

**Prerequisites**

Before starting the integration process, we need to make sure these things are in place.

*   You have an active UpPromote account. If not, please create from [here](https://uppromote.com/).
    
*   The master toggle switch in UpPromote for integration with Loop must be enabled.
    
*   Merchants should be on Loop’s Starter plan or above.
    

If the above prerequisites are met, follow these steps to complete the process.

1.  Navigate to **Loop > Tools & apps > Apps > UpPromote** and click on **Connect**.

Follow these steps to complete the process.

1.  Navigate to **Shopify account > Apps > UpPromote** and click on the **Settings** button.
