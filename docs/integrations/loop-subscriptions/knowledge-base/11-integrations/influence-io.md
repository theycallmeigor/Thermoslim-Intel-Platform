Learn how to integrate Influence.io with Loop to display loyalty points and rewards in the customer portal for a seamless redemption experience.

**[Influence.io](https://influence.io/)** is a comprehensive loyalty, rewards, and referral platform that seamlessly integrates with Loop, empowering you to maximize **customer lifetime value (LTV)** and reduce customer acquisition cost (CAC). This versatile solution enables you to create personalized loyalty programs, reward customers with exclusive perks, and turn one-time buyers into lifelong customers.

### Key features

*   **Earn & Redeem Points**: Implement a flexible rewards system where customers can earn points through purchases, social shares, and more, redeemable for exclusive perks.
    
*   **Referrals**: Convert loyal customers into brand advocates by enabling them to refer friends and family, amplifying your brand’s reach.
    
*   **Loyalty Tiers**: Customize and import existing tier levels to reward your most dedicated customers with VIP benefits, enhancing customer loyalty.  
    ​
    

Influence.io integration is available exclusively on the Loop Pro plan.

* * *

# Integration details

## How does Influence.io work with Loop

This integration enables your customers to apply discount codes that are generated by Influence.io for both initial subscription purchases and recurring subscription orders.

### Prerequisites

*   You should have an active Influence.io account. If not, please create from [here](https://www.influence.io/?utm_source=loopsubscription).

# FAQs

#### Will the discount codes generated for all loyalty programs also apply to subscription purchases?

No, the discount codes generated via subscription rewards only will be applicable for subscriptions.

#### How to change the loyalty widget texts?

You can change the texts by heading over to Loop > Tools & apps > Apps > Influence > "Texts" tab.

#### How do I change the colour of the widget?

The widget can be customised from the customer portal > Themes > styles. You can even add a custom CSS to match your brand's tonality.

#### Which integration is recommended for "Loyalty Points" to reduce cancellations in the customer portal?

While there can be many integrations that are recommended to use Loyalty Points to reduce cancellations in the customer portal, Influence.io might fit your case, being a flexible integration.
