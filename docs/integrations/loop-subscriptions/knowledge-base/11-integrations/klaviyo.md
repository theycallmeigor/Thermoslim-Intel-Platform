Learn how to integrate Klaviyo with Loop to automate targeted emails, track subscription events, and boost retention with personalized marketing campaigns.

**Klaviyo** is a robust third-party marketing automation platform that Loop seamlessly integrates with, enabling you to elevate your customer notifications to the next level. It is a versatile platform that enables users to **effortlessly** create transactional emails, implement effective win-back campaigns for customers who choose to cancel their subscriptions, and specifically target customers who abandon their initial orders.

### Key features

*   Pools data from **subscription events** generated in Loop and triggers transactional email flows based on events such as subscription creation, upcoming orders, payment failures, skipped orders, etc.
    
*   Segment your customers based on the **Loop custom attributes** like active subscriptions count, products subscribed, next order date, etc.
    
*   Create beautiful personalized emails and SMS using pre-designed templates with drag & drop design tools
    
*   Measures the opens, clicks, revenue generated, and breakdown of generated revenue based on custom attributes, and provides trend reports, cohort analysis, and subscriber growth.  
    ​
    

Klaviyo integration is available on both the Loop Starter and Pro plans.

* * *

# Integration details

## How does Klaviyo work with Loop?

Klaviyo integrates with Loop subscriptions to automate targeted email campaigns for subscription-based businesses. By syncing events like sign-ups, order placements, and renewals, Klaviyo triggers personalized email flows that engage subscribers at different stages. This integration helps merchants automate communications, reduce churn, and increase lifetime value (LTV).

**Use case**

**Description**

**Benefit**

**Creating Lists/Segments**

Use Klaviyo to segment subscribers based on their subscription behavior and preferences.

Allows for targeted campaigns, improving engagement and conversion.

**Flows automation**

Use Klaviyo flows to automate subscriber engagement based on subscription events.

Automates personalized communication, improving retention and boosting sales.

**Using quick actions in Klaviyo**

Quick actions can be leveraged with Klaviyo to enhance customer engagement and improve subscription retention.

Send quick-action links that let customers update payment methods, add products, or reactivate subscriptions directly from emails or SMS, no authentication needed.

**Subscriber reactivation campaigns**

Trigger automated reactivation flows when a subscriber is inactive or cancels their subscription.

Reduces churn and re-engages lapsed customers.

FAQs

#### How can we notify customers when their gamification action is executed (for example, adding a free one-time gift)?

To notify customers when their gamification action is executed (for example, adding a free one-time gift), please use the loop\_flow\_completed metric in Klaviyo and create an email flow with that trigger.

#### Does Loop send a Klaviyo event when an order is fully delayed due to out-of-stock or unavailability of a few products? If yes, which event and when is it triggered?

With respect to Klaviyo events, for the Delay and Skip setting at the inventory level, you can create the flow using 2 properties in order/outOfStock payload.

*   "$inventory\_action\_delayed": false/true "$inventory\_action\_skipped": true/false
    

For the partially processed order, use the property under the order/processed payload.

*   "$is\_partially\_processed": false/true
    

#### Is there a log of events passing from Loop > Klaviyo that we can see?

You can check the log of Klaviyo events by going to tools and apps > Klaviyo > integration overview.
