# Update payment methods

Learn how to manage and update customer payment methods in Loop, including editing details or triggering payment update emails from the admin portal.

This guide covers payment update options available for a brand store admin on the Loop admin portal.

## Accessing the current payment method

1. Navigate to Loop > Subscriptions. Search by customer email
2. Open the subscription. The Admin portal view will show all available actions
3. Scroll to the **Payment details** tab on the right side of the portal
4. Customer's current payment details on the contract will be displayed

## Update the current payment method

To update the payment method, the admin can either send an email to the customer or update it themselves if the customer has shared card details.

- Click **"Send link to update payment details"** — an email will be sent to the customer via Shopify
- Click **"Update payment method yourself"** — a form for filling in the card and billing address will open. Paste the details and click "Confirm"
- Click **"Switch to another payment method"** — a list of available payment methods mapped to the customer profile on Shopify will appear. If a subscriber has multiple subscriptions, you can apply the new method to all subscriptions

An admin can also add a new card; after saving, it will appear in the list.

### Ways to get customers to update their payment methods

1. **Send update requests via the Loop admin portal**: Email customers a link prompting them to update their payment details
2. **Enable updates through the customer portal**: Customers can log in and update their payment info directly
3. **Manually update payment info**: If a customer provides their new card and billing details, update their payment method for them or switch to another saved payment method

## FAQs

**Why am I not seeing the option to add or switch to a backup payment method?**
Backup payments are not enabled for your store. The CTAs "Add a backup payment method" and "Switch to backup method" are only visible when backup payments are enabled.

**How to identify the primary payment method?**
Look for the "Primary" chip displayed next to the payment method in the admin portal. The payment method marked with "Primary" is the one that will be used for the upcoming subscription charge.

To change the primary payment method:
1. Go to the customer's subscription in the admin portal
2. Open the payment methods section
3. Select the desired payment method
4. Set it as the primary method
