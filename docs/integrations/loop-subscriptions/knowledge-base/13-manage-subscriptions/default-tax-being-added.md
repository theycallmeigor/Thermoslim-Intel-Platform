# Default tax being added

Learn how Loop handles product taxes and understand why default tax behavior may conflict with external EU tax management apps.

## Overview

Sometimes merchants reach out stating that Loop is adding default tax onto the product price which conflicts with apps they use to manage EU tax rates.

**Key clarification: Loop never interferes with taxes at any point during the order. Taxes are handled entirely by Shopify.**

## Validation

Although Loop never interferes in taxes, you can make the following checks:

1. Check the "Taxes and Duties" tab in Shopify's settings to see if any tax is applied
2. Go to the markets tab to see if there is a difference in markets or currency conversion causing the price difference

## Resolution

1. As per market preferences, Tax (VAT) is added based on the customer's country. For example, the VAT rate in EU countries (e.g., Germany) is 19% for products manufactured in the UK.
   - Reference: [Shopify Dynamic Tax-Inclusive Pricing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/pricing/dynamic-tax-inclusive-pricing)

2. Reach out to Shopify Support — Taxes and duties are handled by Shopify, not Loop. Loop has confirmation from Shopify's Merchant Tax Expert that prices and taxes are completely controlled by Shopify Settings.
