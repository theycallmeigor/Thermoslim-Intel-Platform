Learn how to use Loop Campaigns to engage subscribers with personalized offers, drive product discovery, and boost retention through in-portal and email campaigns.

Campaigns help you reach your subscribers through timely, personalized offers directly inside the **Customer Portal** or via **Email/SMS**.

They’re designed to promote discovery, trigger engagement, and simplify subscriber actions from adding a new product to resuming a paused subscription.

All campaigns are integrated with third party marketing platforms such as **Klaviyo** giving brands complete control over how and when they reach subscribers.

* * *

# Why use campaigns?

Campaigns help brands drive engagement and retention through highly visible, action-oriented offers.  
​

Key benefits:

*   **Higher engagement:** Drive offer visibility by displaying banners in the customer portal rather than relying solely on email opens.
    
*   **Boost revenue & AOV:** Encourage customers to add complementary or premium products to their subscriptions.
    
*   **Create urgency:** Set limited-time deals to prompt faster decisions and increased conversions.
    
*   **Better retention:** Convert paused or cancelled subscriptions back to active ones through personalized win-back offers.
    

  
​

# Types of campaigns

Each campaign type is designed to achieve a specific business goal.

**Campaign type**

**Description**

**Use case**

**Upsell/Cross-sell product**

Unlock exclusive products at special prices for loyal subscribers

New product launch • Exclusive subscriber offer for rare product • Stock clearance campaign for specific product

**Recover failed payments**

Incentivize subscribers to update their failing payment methods

Reduce churn from payment failures

**Winback campaign**

Re-engage cancelled subscribers with irresistible one-click offers

Regain churned subscribers

**Resume subscription**

Motivate paused subscribers to resume with discounts

“Welcome back” incentive for seasonal or paused users

**Swap products**

Replace out-of-stock items with alternatives instantly

Prevent cancellations due to OOS/discontinued SKUs • Introduce higher-margin or better-fit substitutes

**Order now**

Encourage customers to place orders sooner

Allow early ordering to create urgency or meet demand spikes

**Login campaign**

Encourage portal logins for exclusive offers

Highlight rewards to improve subscriber portal engagement

# Key features

1.  **Customer portal banners**  
    A new discovery channel to showcase live offers directly on the Customer Portal.  
    ​
    
    *   Subscribers can view and engage with ongoing campaigns instantly.
        
    *   Clicking the banner opens an in-portal offer drawer to explore and accept the offer.
        
    *   Once accepted, the campaign is marked as completed and removed for that subscription.
        
    *   If multiple campaigns apply, subscribers can swipe through multiple banners.  
        ​  
        ​**Banner customization path:**  
        Loop Admin → Customer Portal → Themes → \[Your Theme\] → Customize → Layout → Subscription Details Page → Campaign Banner  
        ​
        
    
2.  **Limited-time offers**  
    Create urgency by setting an expiry date for your campaign.  
    ​
    
    *   Once expired, the banner disappears automatically from the portal.
        
    *   Any expired link (shared via email or SMS) will redirect subscribers to the portal but won’t show the offer drawer.  
        ​  
        ​**Note**:All the campaign types will be limited time only. Brands can run the campaigns for 2 months (difference between campaign start and end date)  
        ​
        
    
3.  **Campaign templates**  
    To make it easier for brands to get started, Loop provides ready templates for various use cases:  
    ​
    
    *   **New product launch** – Announce and sell new products to existing subscribers.
        
    *   **Exclusive subscriber offer** – Offer early access or subscriber-only deals.
        
    *   **Stock clearance** – Clear inventory efficiently by promoting select SKUs.  
        ​
        
    

**Schedule campaigns:** No more last minute checklist hassle to turn on subscription campaigns. Brands can effortlessly schedule campaign to a future date and time to match the offer timings.

# How to create a campaign

Follow these simple steps to set up your campaign. Let's say upsell/cross-sell product campaihm:

1.  **Navigate to:**  
    ​Loop Admin → Campaigns → Create Campaign → Upsell/Cross-sell Product  
    ​
    
2.  **Add an internal name** for easy identification.  
    ​
    
3.  **Select audience:**
    
    *   All active subscriptions or
        
    *   Specific subscriptions using filters (e.g., frequency, product, or customer tags)
        
        ​
        
    
4.  **Select channel:**
    
    *   Customer Portal banner
        
    *   Email/SMS via Klaviyo or other integrations
        
    *   Both  
        ​
        
    
5.  **Select product:**  
    Choose the product you wish to promote as an upsell or cross-sell.
    
6.  **Add banner images:**
    
    *   Desktop banner (recommended: 3912 × 964 px)
        
    *   Mobile banner (recommended: 2000 × 960 px)
        
    
7.  **Customize offer texts:**  
    Edit button labels and success messages like:
    
    Offer Details, Accept Offer, Decline Offer, and success messages  
    ​
    
8.  **Set timeline:**  
    Add start and end dates to define the campaign’s active duration.
    
9.  **Notify via Email**_(Optional)_**:**  
    Enter an email to receive completion alerts.
    
10.  Click **Save** to launch your campaign.

# Customer experience

Once live, subscribers visiting the **Customer portal** will see banners (if created) for ongoing campaigns.

*   Clicking the banner opens an offer drawer to explore the details.
    
*   They can choose to:
    
    *   **Add as Subscription Product**, or
        
    *   **Add as One-time Add-on**
        
    
*   Upon completion, a success message confirms the product addition, and the campaign disappears from their view.
    

This real-time experience ensures subscribers don’t miss exclusive deals while reducing friction compared to traditional email-only promotions.

# Performance tracking

You can view the performance of each campaign under **Loop Admin → Campaigns**

Metrics available:

*   **Audience:** Number of subscribers targeted.
    
*   **Clicks:** Number of subscribers who interacted with the campaign.
    
*   **Completion rate:** Percentage of subscribers who completed the desired action.
    

These insights help brands evaluate which campaigns are driving the most engagement and identify opportunities to optimize offer design or targeting.

# FAQs

#### What happens when a campaign expires?

The banner automatically disappears, and the offer links no longer open the campaign drawer.

#### Can multiple campaigns be visible at once?

Yes, subscribers can view and swipe through multiple active campaign banners on their portal.

#### Can I run upsell campaigns alongside recover failed payment or winback campaigns?

Yes, campaigns can run simultaneously and target overlapping audiences without any conflict.

#### Do campaigns support external marketing tools like Klaviyo?

Yes, you can sync and send campaign links via Klaviyo.

#### What are campaign actions?

Campaign Actions are used for events that have already occurred.

Campaign actions are used when you need to generate a quick action URL for a particular event in bulk. This applies when the event has already occurred, such as when customers have already canceled their subscriptions. In this case, we would initiate a campaign for all customers who canceled their subscriptions in the past. Campaign actions are used within the Klaviyo platform.
