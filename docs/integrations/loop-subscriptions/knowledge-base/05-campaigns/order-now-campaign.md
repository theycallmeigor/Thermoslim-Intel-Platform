Create urgency and reward subscribers with exciting offers to place their next order instantly

  
The **Order now** campaign allows brands to encourage subscribers to place their upcoming order immediately instead of waiting for the scheduled billing date.

This campaign is ideal when you want to accelerate revenue, create urgency around limited-time offers, or drive higher engagement during peak demand periods.  
​

##   
Why use Order now campaigns?

Order now campaigns help brands drive immediate action from active subscribers.

**Key benefits:**  
​

*   Higher cash flow: Accelerate upcoming revenue by encouraging early orders.
    
*   Create urgency: Run time-bound offers to push faster decisions.
    
*   Boost engagement: Re-activate passive subscribers who may not frequently visit the portal.
    
*   Clear inventory strategically: Promote immediate purchases during stock clearance or seasonal pushes.
    

##   
When to use this campaign?

This campaign works best in scenarios like:

• Limited-time discount for placing the order today  
• Festive or flash sale for subscribers only  
• Inventory clearance with immediate purchase incentive  
• End-of-month revenue push  
• Demand spike planning

###   
Important prerequisite

The **Order now** action works only when order placement via customer portal is enabled.

If disabled, you will see a message prompting you to enable:

Customer Portal → Preferences → Place order preference

Enable this to allow subscribers to place their order via campaigns.

##   

Follow these steps to set up your campaign:

Navigate to:  
Loop Admin → Campaigns → Create Campaign → Order now

Add an internal name for easy identification.  
​  
Select audience

Choose who should see this campaign:

• All Active subscriptions  
• Specific Active subscriptions using filters (frequency, product, customer tags, etc.)  
• Add specific subscription IDs

You can combine filters to create precise targeting.

#### Select channel

Choose where subscribers will see this campaign:

• Customer portal banner  
• Email/SMS via third-party marketing apps (Klaviyo)  
• Both customer portal and Email/SMS

#### Select incentive (Optional)

You can optionally reward subscribers for placing the order now:

• Add free gift  
• Add discount  
• None

Incentives increase conversion rates by adding immediate value.

#### **Add customer portal banners**

Upload creatives to notify subscribers:

The banner appears inside the subscription details page.

#### **Customize texts**

Configure campaign messaging. Clear communication reduces friction and improves acceptance rate.

#### **Set timeline**

Set the campaign start and end date and time.

Note: Campaign duration by default is 1 month (difference between start and end date).  
Once expired:

• Banner disappears automatically  
• Expired links will redirect to portal but not open the campaign drawer

#### **Notify completion alert (Optional)**

Enter an email address to receive notifications when subscribers complete the campaign action.

To know how to set up campaigns in Klaviyo, [click here](https://help.loopwork.co/en/articles/12733587-popular-use-cases-in-klaviyo#h_25bf707b09)

When the campaign is live:

Eligible subscribers visiting the Customer Portal will see the banner.

On clicking the banner:

• A popup appears opens with offer window  
• They can place their order immediately  
• Incentives (if configured) are automatically applied

After completion:

• A success message confirms the order  
• The campaign disappears for that subscription

This creates a seamless, one-click acceleration of the subscriber journey.  
​

##   
Performance tracking

Track campaign results under:  
Loop Admin → Campaigns

Available metrics:

Audience – Total targeted subscriptions  
Clicks – Subscribers who interacted  
Completion rate – Subscribers who placed the order

Use these insights to optimize targeting, messaging, and incentives.  
​

##   
FAQs

**What happens to the subscriber’s original billing date?**

The subscription continues as per normal schedule after the order is placed. The next order date recalculates based on the subscription frequency.

**Can I run Order now alongside other campaigns?**

Yes. If multiple campaigns are active, subscribers can swipe between banners in the portal.

**What happens if the campaign expires?**

The banner disappears automatically and subscribers cannot access the offer.

**Does Order now support Klaviyo?**

Yes. You can send campaign-triggered emails or SMS via third-party integrations like Klaviyo.
