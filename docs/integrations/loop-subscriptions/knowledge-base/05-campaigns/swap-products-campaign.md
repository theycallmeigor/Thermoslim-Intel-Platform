Prevent revenue loss from low inventory or discontinued SKUs by offering subscribers a seamless, one-click product swap inside customer portal.

The **Swap products** campaign allows brands to replace one or more existing subscription products with recommended alternatives without requiring subscribers to cancel or manually edit their subscription.

This is especially powerful for managing low inventory, product discontinuations, or introducing higher-margin alternatives.  
​

##   
Why use Swap products campaigns?

Swap campaigns help brands proactively protect subscription revenue.  
​

**Key benefits:**

*   Prevent cancellations: Offer a ready alternative instead of forcing subscribers to cancel due to OOS products.
    
*   Protect revenue: Avoid churn caused by discontinued or low-stock SKUs.
    
*   Promote better-fit products: Recommend upgraded, reformulated, or higher-margin alternatives.
    
*   Support complex mappings: Configure many-to-many product mappings (multiple old products to multiple new product options).
    

##   
When should you use this campaign?

This campaign works best in situations like:

• Product discontinued permanently  
• Temporary stock-out  
• Reformulated or upgraded version launch  
• SKU rationalization  
• Introducing premium alternatives  
​

##   
Many-to-many product mapping (Old → New)

The Swap products campaign supports flexible mapping:

• One old product → Multiple new product options  
• Multiple old products → One new product  
• Multiple old products → Multiple new products

This allows brands to handle bulk product migrations and complex catalog transitions smoothly.  
​

Example:

**Old products:**  
Protein Vanilla 500g  
Protein Chocolate 500g

**New products:**  
Protein Vanilla 1kg  
Protein Chocolate 1kg  
Premium Blend Protein  
​

Subscribers can choose from recommended alternatives based on your configuration.  
​

##   
How to create a Swap products campaign

Navigate to:  
Loop Admin → Campaigns → Create Campaign → Swap products

Add an internal name for easy identification.

#### Select audience

Choose the subscriptions that should see the swap offer:

• All subscriptions  
• Specific subscriptions

*   Filter by conditions (product, frequency, tags, etc.)
    
*   Add specific subscription IDs
    

Targeting ensures only affected subscribers see the swap campaign.

#### Select channel

Choose where the swap will be displayed:

• Customer portal banner  
• Email/SMS via 3rd party marketing apps (Klaviyo)  
• Both customer portal and Email/SMS

#### Configure Action properties

**Step 1: Select old products (any of)**

Select the products that will be swapped.

These are the products currently present in the subscriber’s active subscription.

  
​**Step 2: Select new product(s)**

Choose the replacement product(s) to recommend.

You can also set priority if multiple new products are added.

Subscribers will see these as swap options in the drawer.

  
​**Step 3: Define pricing behavior**

Choose how pricing should be handled:

• Keep the price and discounts same as that of the old product(s)  
• Use the price of the new product and apply existing discounts  
• Use the price of the new product and apply discounts from its associated selling plan  
• Use the price of the new product and apply a custom discount

This gives full control over revenue and margin impact during swaps.

#### Add customer portal banners

Upload creatives to notify subscribers:

The banner appears inside the subscription details page.

#### Customize texts

Configure campaign messaging. Clear communication reduces friction and improves acceptance rate.

#### Set timeline

Set the campaign start and end date and time.

Note: Campaign duration by default is 1 month (difference between start and end date).  
Once expired:

• Banner disappears automatically  
• Swap drawer cannot be accessed

#### Notify completion alert (Optional)

Enter an email address to receive alerts when subscribers complete swaps.  
​

​  
When the campaign is live:

Eligible subscribers will see a banner in their customer portal if set.

On clicking:

• A swap drawer opens  
• Existing product is displayed  
• Recommended new product options are shown  
• Subscriber can review product details  
• One-click confirm completes the swap  
​

Once confirmed:

• Subscription updates immediately  
• Success message is displayed  
• Campaign disappears for that subscription

This prevents manual edits and avoids cancellation-driven churn.  
​

##   
Performance tracking

Track results under:  
Loop Admin → Campaigns

Metrics available:

Audience – Total subscriptions targeted  
Clicks – Subscribers who opened the swap drawer  
Completion rate – Successful product swaps

Use these insights to measure acceptance rate and optimize replacement strategy.  
​

##   
FAQs

#### Can I map multiple old products to multiple new products?

Yes. Swap campaigns support many-to-many product mapping.

#### What happens to discounts after a swap?

It depends on the pricing rule selected during configuration.

#### Can I combine Swap products with other campaigns?

No. Only the product changes — frequency and billing schedule remain intact.
