Win back churned subscribers quickly with limited-time, one-click reactivation offers.

  
The **Winback campaign** helps brands re-engage subscribers who have cancelled their subscriptions by offering a time-bound incentive to reactivate. It’s designed to reduce churn impact by making reactivation simple and action-oriented, either through the Customer Portal experience or through personalized links shared via Email/SMS using third-party marketing tools.  
​

##   
Why use Winback campaigns?

Winback campaigns help brands recover lost subscription revenue by making it easier for cancelled subscribers to return.  
​

**Key benefits:**

*   Recover churned subscribers: Bring back cancelled subscribers with a clear, time-bound reactivation offer.
    
*   Reduce friction: Subscribers can reactivate with a one-click confirmation flow.
    
*   Create urgency: Limited-time timelines encourage faster action.
    
*   Improve conversion with incentives: Add a discount or free gift to increase reactivation rate.
    
*   Flexible outreach: Share winback actions via CSV quick action links or sync them to Klaviyo.  
    ​
    

##   
How Winback works

When a subscriber clicks a Winback campaign link, they are redirected to the Customer Portal and immediately shown a reactivation confirmation drawer. Once they confirm, their subscription is reactivated and any configured incentive is applied automatically.  
​

How to create a Winback campaign

Navigate to: Loop Admin → Campaigns → Create Campaign → Winback

Add an internal name for easy identification.

#### Select audience

Choose who should receive the winback offer:

All Cancelled subscriptions, or

Specific Cancelled subscriptions

The system will show the total subscriptions selected.

#### Select incentive

Choose the incentive for reactivation:

*   Add free gift
    
*   Add discount
    
*   None
    

If configured, incentives are applied once the subscriber reactivates.

#### Choose how to share campaign actions

You can distribute winback actions using:

*   Generate manual quick action list  
    A CSV will be generated containing personalized reactivation URLs. This list can be used to trigger campaigns using third-party marketing tools.
    

*   Sync campaign action list to Klaviyo  
    Reactivation links are synced directly to Klaviyo for streamlined outreach.
    

#### Customize texts

Configure the texts shown to subscribers in the reactivation drawer. Clear communication reduces friction and improves acceptance rate.

#### Set timeline

Set the campaign start and end date and time.

Note: Campaign duration by default is 1 month (difference between start and end date). Once expired, reactivation links will no longer work.

#### Notify completion alert (Optional)

Enter an email address to receive alerts when subscribers complete the winback action.

To know how to set up campaigns in Klaviyo, [click here](https://help.loopwork.co/en/articles/12733587-popular-use-cases-in-klaviyo#h_25bf707b09)

​

##   
Performance tracking

You can view the performance of each campaign under Loop Admin → Campaigns.

Metrics available:

Audience: Number of subscribers targeted.

Clicks: Number of subscribers who interacted with the campaign.

Completion rate: Percentage of subscribers who successfully reactivated.  
​

##   
FAQs

#### Can I target only a specific set of cancelled subscriptions?

Yes. Use Specific Cancelled subscriptions while setting the audience.

#### Does Winback support Klaviyo?

Yes. You can sync winback action links directly to Klaviyo, or export them via the manual quick action list.

#### What happens when a winback campaign expires?

Once expired, the campaign links stop working and subscribers can’t access the reactivation drawer.
