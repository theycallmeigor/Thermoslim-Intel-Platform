# Payment analytics V1

Provides insights into payment performance, recovery efforts, and failure trends. It is essential for managing the subscription payment lifecycle and identifying revenue risks.

### Key Metrics

*   **Payments Snapshot:** Total Attempted, Success, Recovered, and Lost revenue (Last 30 Days).
*   **Success vs. Failed Revenue:** Monthly payment health over the last 12 months.
*   **Recovery Analytics:** Performance by method (card update vs. retry) and failure reason.
*   **Upcoming Payments:** Monitoring at-risk payments based on expiration or remaining retries.
