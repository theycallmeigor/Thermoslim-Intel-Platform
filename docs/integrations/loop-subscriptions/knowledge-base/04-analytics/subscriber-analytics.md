# Subscriber analytics

This article explains Loop's enhanced analytics for tracking subscriber growth, behavior, churn, and revenue. Key highlights include the ability to see the full picture of the subscriber base, focus on upsells, and monitor acquisition quality with 0-day churn tracking.

### Key Metrics

*   **Subscriber-level analytics:** Acquisition tracking by product, plan, and frequency.
*   **Revenue Insights:** Subscriber vs. non-subscriber revenue; recurring vs. checkout performance.
*   **Growth & Churn:** Visualization of changes (New, Resumed, Reactivated, Paused, Canceled, and Expired).
*   **LTV Insights:** Deep dive into Lifetime Value segments (e.g., customers who subscribed on their 1st, 2nd, or 3rd+ order).
