# Cancellation analytics

Teaches merchants how to understand why and when subscribers cancel. It distinguishes between full subscriber churn and individual subscription cancellations.

### Key Metrics

*   **Churn Overview & Trends:** High-level trends over time.
*   **Product-Level Cancellations:** Which products drive the most churn.
*   **Order-Wise Cancellations:** Where in the customer journey churn happens (e.g., after 1st or 2nd order).
*   **Reason-Wise Cancellations:** Distribution of reasons like "pricing" or "inventory surplus."
*   **Save Metrics:** Effectiveness of cancellation flows and save actions.
