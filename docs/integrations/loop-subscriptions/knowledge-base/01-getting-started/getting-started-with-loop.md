# What are subscriptions?

Subscriptions improve customer repeat purchase rates. When a customer subscribes to a product or service, their card details are saved by Shopify and they are billed automatically based on the billing frequency defined in the selling plan.  
​

Instead of purchasing a product multiple times and going through checkout repeatedly, subscribers receive automatic deliveries at regular intervals, such as every week or every month.

# Types of subscriptions

There are several subscription models you can implement with Loop. The table below describes each type with links to detailed setup guides.

**Subscription type**

**Description**

One-time and subscription

Customers can purchase a product as a one-time purchase or with a recurring frequency.

[Subscription only](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12716819-hide-one-time-purchase-option)

Customers can only purchase through a recurring subscription (no one-time purchase option).

[Prepaid subscription](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12731270-prepaid-subscription)

Customers make a single upfront payment for goods or services delivered on a repeat basis.

[Gift subscription](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12729851-gift-subscriptions)

An extension of prepaid subscriptions where customers pay upfront for a set duration to gift to someone else.

Membership subscriptions

Customers subscribe to a membership or loyalty club, offering exclusive benefits and perks.

[Trial subscriptions](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12731234-trial-subscription)

Customers test the product for a set duration before being billed on a recurring basis.

[Build a box](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12729045-loop-bundles)

Customers build their own bundle and subscribe to it.

# Setting up subscriptions

## Prerequisites

Before setting up subscriptions, ensure you have:

*   The [Loop app](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-subscriptions) installed on your Shopify store. If you haven't installed it yet, [click here](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-subscriptions) to install.
    
*   One of Shopify's supported payment providers enabled. For a complete list of compatible payment gateways, visit the [Shopify help center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/setup#eligibility-requirements).
    

## Setup steps

Once the prerequisites are complete, follow these steps to set up subscriptions:

1.  [Create selling plans](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12674241-selling-plans): Define your subscription model, including billing frequencies, customer discounts, and other terms to attract and retain subscribers.
    
2.  [Map products to selling plans](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12716791-mapping-products-to-selling-plan): Assign products to the appropriate selling plans, making them available for subscription at specified frequencies.
    
3.  [Install the Loop widget](https://intercom.help/loop-subscriptions/en/articles/12729262-loop-widget-set-up): Enable the Loop subscription widget on your product pages so customers can easily subscribe.
    
4.  Configure subscription settings: Customize your subscription settings based on your desired subscription type and business needs.

# FAQs

#### I would like to start using Loop from scratch, how should I go about it?

To set up Loop from scratch on your Shopify store:

1.  Install Loop on your Shopify store.
    
2.  Create a selling plan.
    
3.  Map products to the selling plan.
    
4.  Install the widget to your Shopify theme.
    

For detailed instructions, refer to our knowledge articles at [help.loopwork.co](https://help.loopwork.co). Upgrade to a relevant Loop plan to access more features.

#### Will my subscriptions be cancelled if I uninstall Loop?

If you uninstall Loop, your existing active subscriptions stop processing. No further orders are placed, and subscriptions are marked as cancelled automatically.
