# Checkout links

Single-click URLs that direct customers to a pre-filled checkout page.

### Use Cases

*   Email marketing campaigns (e.g., Klaviyo).
*   QR codes for physical marketing materials.
*   Simplifying the purchase process for repeat buyers.

### Setup

1.  Navigate to **Acquire > Checkout links > Create**.
2.  Select products, quantities, and selling plans.
3.  Add optional discount codes and UTM parameters.
4.  Generate and copy the unique URL.

### Tracking Metrics

*   Total clicks
*   Overall conversion rate
*   Checkout revenue
*   MRR added

### Considerations

*   Links lock in selling plans at the time of creation; plan updates require generating new links.
*   Not available for prepaid plans or Build-Your-Own-Bundle (BYOB) bundles.
