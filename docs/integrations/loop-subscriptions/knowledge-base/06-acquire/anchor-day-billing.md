# Anchor day billing

Charges all subscribers on a specific day/date (e.g., the 1st of every month) regardless of their sign-up date.

### Use Cases

*   Digital subscriptions
*   Meal boxes with specific delivery cutoffs
*   Fitness memberships

### Setup

1.  Open the **Selling plan** in Loop Admin.
2.  Click **"View details"** in the "Set order schedule" section.
3.  Enable **"Do you charge on specific day...?"**.
4.  Select the desired day (weekly, monthly, or yearly).

### Terminologies

*   **Anchor Day:** The specific day/date for billing.
*   **First Order Placement:** "On checkout" (immediate) or "On anchor" (scheduled).
*   **Cut-off Window:** Skips the first anchor if the sign-up is too close to it.
