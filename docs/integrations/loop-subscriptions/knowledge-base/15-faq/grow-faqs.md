# Upsell

#### If a product becomes out of inventory/store, will it automatically be hidden from the upsell section in Loop as well?

To manage a product if it goes out of the store/inventory, you can check your setup at: Loop > Grow > Upsell > Preference

#### Do products in smart collections on Shopify get synced into the Loop automatically?

No, products in smart collections on Shopify don't get synced into the Loop automatically?
