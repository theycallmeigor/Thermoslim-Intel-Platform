#### Does the "completion rate" for campaigns mean they added the product to their subscription?

Completion rate in Analytics measured the percentage of completion based on the clicks count, like what percentage of customers clicked on the link completed the actual action.

#### How do I view the uploaded csv list for a Campaign action?

To view the uploaded CSV, please go to Loop > Campaigns and open the campaign you want to check. You’ll see a link labeled “Uploaded CSV file.” Click on it, and the file will download in CSV format.

#### How to run campaign actions in Loop app?

You can run the campaign actions for customers in the Loop app by going to Loop > Campaign > Campaign actions.  
From here, you can send emails to customers that allow them to add a product as a subscription to their existing subscription. You can also target subscribers based on segments, as per your choice.  
​  
To achieve something similar to the upsell bar in Klaviyo-sent emails, you can use Zaymo.  
With Zaymo, all the steps — like skipping an order, placing an order now, or upgrading — can be handled directly within the email itself.

#### When creating a campaign, does the condition “Completed subscription orders” include the first order placed by the customer at checkout?

The “Completed subscription orders” condition counts the initial subscription order placed at checkout as a completed subscription order.

#### How do I send direct login links to my customers via email so they can access the portal without logging in?

To send login links for Customer portal to customers in bulk, please use Campaign Actions by going to the: Loop app > Campaigns > click Create a Campaign and choose “Login” as the action.

From there, select the customers you want to target using conditions. Then either generate a manual quick action list or choose to auto-sync the campaign action link to Klaviyo.

After saving, you’ll be able to download a CSV file containing all the quick login URLs for each customer. Upload this file to Klaviyo, and you can send your email campaign from there.

#### How can I set up multiple product mappings in a swap campaign?  

To set up flexible product mappings in a swap campaign, you can map multiple existing products to multiple new products within a single campaign. You can also select specific variants while creating these mappings, allowing you to precisely control which variants subscribers are allowed to swap to.  
​

The Swap products campaign supports flexible mapping:

• One old product → Multiple new product options  
• Multiple old products → One new product  
• Multiple old products → Multiple new products

This allows brands to handle bulk product migrations and complex catalog transitions smoothly. To manage and update swap product priorities and mappings, you can edit the priority order and modify mapped products directly during campaign creation. Once campaign is created, you cannot modify the products list.  
​

#### How can I target subscribers who do not have a specific product in their active subscription for upsell or cross-sell campaigns?

To target subscribers who do not have a specific product in their active subscription, follow these steps:

1.  Go to Campaigns in your admin.
    
2.  Create a new campaign or edit an existing one.
    
3.  In the audience conditions section, select the “Products not in subscription” condition.
    
4.  Choose the product you want to promote.
    
5.  Configure the rest of your campaign and publish it.
    

You can use this condition to ensure that only subscribers who have not added the selected product to their active subscription are included in your upsell, cross-sell, or win-back campaign.

#### How can I filter subscribers based on the total number of subscription orders in a campaign?

To filter subscribers based on the total number of subscription orders, follow these steps:

1.  Go to Campaigns in your admin.
    
2.  Create or edit a campaign.
    
3.  In the audience conditions section, select “Total subscription orders.”
    
4.  Set the value or rule you want to apply.
    
5.  Save and launch your campaign.
    

You can use this condition to target subscribers based on how many subscription orders they have placed.
