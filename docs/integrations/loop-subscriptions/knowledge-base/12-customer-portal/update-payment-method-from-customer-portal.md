# Update payment method from customer portal

Learn how customers can manage and update their payment methods in Loop's customer portal for seamless and uninterrupted subscription billing.

This guide covers payment update options available for a customer on their subscription management customer portal. Same as the admin portal, customers can add a new card, update the card or billing address, and switch between cards.

## Customer portal payment preferences

Loop allows you to control whether customers can update their payment details on the portal. You can enable or disable this feature from: **Loop admin > Customer portal > Preferences > Payment and Shipping Details**.

## Update the current payment method

If enabled, the customer will see the **Update** or **Change** button in the payment details section on the customer portal. Clicking **"Update"** allows editing the current card and billing address.

## Switching to a new payment method

When the customer clicks **"Change"** on the customer portal in the payment section, a list of all the cards mapped to the customer profile on Shopify will appear, along with an option to **"Apply this payment method to all subscriptions"**.

Customers can also add a new card by entering card details and billing address. The new card will then appear in the payment method list.
