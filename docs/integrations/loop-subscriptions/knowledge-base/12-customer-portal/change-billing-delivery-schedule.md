# Change billing/delivery schedule

Learn how subscribers can easily adjust delivery frequencies in Loop, ensuring flexibility and convenience without needing support assistance.

Subscribers may want to adjust how often they receive their subscriptions — for example, switching from a 1-month to a 2-month delivery cycle. Loop makes this process effortless through its intuitive customer portal, allowing subscribers to update their delivery frequency on their own, without needing to contact support.

## Enabling the setting

1. Navigate to **Loop > Customer portal > Preferences > Subscription action section > Edit billing/delivery schedule setting**
2. Enable this setting to allow customers to edit their billing or delivery frequency directly from the customer portal. You can also define the maximum interval that can be selected.

> The subscription discount will remain unchanged even if different discounts are configured for different delivery frequencies. To automatically recalculate the discount based on the newly selected frequency, enable the second setting shown in the screenshot.

## Customer portal experience

Once enabled, the customer will see an Edit button allowing them to choose between the delivery intervals you have set up on the selling plans.

1. Customer logs in to the customer portal, opens the desired subscription, and clicks the "Edit" button in the Subscription plan section
2. The edit plan drawer opens. Customer selects the desired frequency interval from the given choices
   - **Note:** The next order date can be changed by clicking on the displayed date and selecting a new one from the calendar. By default, next order date shown is based on the previously set frequency schedule.
3. On clicking Confirm, the new frequency plan is applied and the discount is recalculated accordingly

## Admin portal experience

To change the frequency plan on behalf of a subscriber:

1. Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions** and search for the customer with "Subscription ID" in the Subscriptions tab
2. Click on the "Edit" button in the Subscription details section
3. The Edit subscription details drawer opens. Admin can update: frequency, next order date, billing hour, recalculate discounts per new frequency, anchor day, and more. Click Save.
