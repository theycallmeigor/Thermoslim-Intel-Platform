# Update shipping details

Learn how customers and merchants can update shipping addresses in Loop to ensure accurate deliveries and a smooth subscription experience.

## From the Loop customer portal

**Prerequisites:**
1. Customer must have an active subscription on the app
2. "Update Shipping Address" must be enabled at: **Customer portal > Preferences > Payment and Shipping Details**

Once enabled, the customer sees an "edit" button in the shipping details section. Clicking it opens the shipping address form.

## From the Loop admin portal

**Prerequisite:** Customer must have a subscription on Loop.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions** and search for the customer with "Subscription ID"
2. In the "Shipping Address" section, click "Edit"
3. Change the address accordingly and click "Save"
