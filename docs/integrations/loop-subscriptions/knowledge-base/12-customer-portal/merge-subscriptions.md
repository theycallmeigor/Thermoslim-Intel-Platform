# Merge subscriptions

Learn how to merge multiple subscriptions in Loop to simplify management, align deliveries, and reduce shipping costs for a seamless customer experience.

Customers may want to merge subscriptions to:
- Align delivery dates for convenience
- Reduce shipping fees by combining deliveries
- Manage fewer subscription records
- Maintain consistency in billing frequency and address

## Prerequisites

- Subscriptions must share the **same shipping address** and have **identical billing and delivery frequencies**
- The customer must have active subscriptions eligible to be merged
- Once the order date is selected, all future deliveries will be aligned based on the delivery frequency of the merged subscription

## Enabling the setting

Navigate to **Customer portal > Preferences > Subscription actions > Enable the merge subscriptions checkbox**

## Merging from the customer portal

1. Navigate to the customer portal and open the subscription listing screen
2. Locate two active subscriptions with the same delivery frequency (e.g., 1 month)
3. Click the **Merge subscriptions** button
4. In the dialog box, select the desired shipping address and click Next
5. Select the subscriptions you want to merge, then click "Choose order date"
6. Choose the desired date from the calendar and click Confirm
7. The two subscriptions are consolidated into one — both products are mapped to the unified subscription; the original subscription is marked as expired

> The shipping rates will be recalculated based on the shipping profile settings and applied to the final subscription.

## Things to keep in mind

Subscriptions **cannot** be merged if:
1. One subscription contains a digital product and the other contains a physical product
2. One subscription is enrolled in a streak

In these cases, the merge subscription CTA will be hidden on the subscription listing page on the customer portal.
