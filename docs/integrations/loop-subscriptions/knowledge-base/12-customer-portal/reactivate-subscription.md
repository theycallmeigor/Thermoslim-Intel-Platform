# Reactivate subscription

Learn how to easily reactivate cancelled subscriptions in Loop, helping brands win back subscribers and strengthen long-term customer relationships.

A customer may reactivate a previously cancelled subscription for reasons such as finding continued value in the product, resolving temporary financial or personal issues, or receiving a special incentive. Reactivation helps maintain long-term customer relationships and offers a seamless way to bring back loyal subscribers without requiring them to set up a new subscription from scratch.

## Prerequisites

- The customer should have already purchased and cancelled at least one subscription from the store in the past

## Enabling the setting

1. Navigate to **Customer portal > Preferences > Subscription actions > Enable the reactivate subscription option**
2. If you wish to charge the customer immediately upon reactivation, select that option and configure additional options as needed
3. You can also choose to apply this setting for anchor-based subscriptions

> If the setting is disabled, customers cannot reactivate from the customer portal — only the admin portal can do so.

## Reactivating from the customer portal

1. Customer navigates to the Shopify store via login details or email link
2. Find the cancelled subscription under inactive/completed subscriptions and click the "view details" icon
3. Once all information is verified, click the **Reactivate** button in the top-right corner
4. A reactivate subscription pop-up appears — click Confirm

## Reactivating from the admin portal

1. Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions**, then open the cancelled subscription to reactivate
2. Click **Reactivate** at the top right of the screen
3. A pop-up appears — choose whether to immediately place an order on reactivation, choose to notify the customer, then click Confirm
