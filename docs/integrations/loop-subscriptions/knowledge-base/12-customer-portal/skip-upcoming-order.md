# Skip upcoming order

Learn how to enable and manage order skips in Loop, giving subscribers flexibility while helping brands reduce cancellations and improve retention.

Skipping an upcoming order allows subscribers to pause a scheduled delivery without cancelling their subscription. With Loop, subscribers can easily skip their next scheduled delivery directly from the customer and admin portal.

## Enabling the skip option

1. Navigate to **Loop admin > Customer portal > Preferences > Order actions > Skip order**
2. Enable this setting to allow customers to skip upcoming orders directly from the customer portal
3. Optionally, limit the maximum number of consecutive skips allowed per subscription to prevent revenue loss

## How the skip feature works

When the skip function is used on an upcoming subscription order, the order is marked as skipped on both the customer and admin portal.

A skipped order can be **unskipped anytime** before the scheduled date.

Skipped orders will remain in the skipped state unless the admin or customer performs one of these actions:
- Place order
- Reschedule order
- Delay order
- Edit billing/delivery schedule
- Pause / Resume subscription

## Skipping from the customer portal

**Prerequisites:**
1. The customer must have at least one active subscription
2. The "Skip order" option must be enabled

### Partial skip (Loop Pro only)

Loop smartly recognizes if the customer has multiple items in their subscription and nudges them to skip only selected items from their order — minimizing revenue loss while giving customers flexibility.

1. Subscriber logs in and clicks the "Skip next order" button
2. If they have multiple products, a drawer opens with a list of products
3. Customer selects which products to skip from the upcoming order and clicks "Skip selected items" to confirm
4. The selected items are removed from the upcoming order

> Partial skip is **not shown** to customers when:
> - There is only one product in the subscription
> - There is only one paid product and all others are free
> - The subscription has bundle or prepaid products

> **Note:** Partial skip option is only available on **Loop Pro plan**. Free or Starter plan customers see the Complete order skip experience.

### Complete order skip

Click **Skip** and press Confirm.

## Skipping from the admin portal

1. Navigate to Loop admin and search for the customer's "Subscription ID" in the Subscriptions tab
2. Open the subscription, scroll down to the Order Schedule tab and click **Skip order** on the desired order
3. Optionally select "Notify customer" to send an email notification from Loop, then click Confirm

## FAQs

**Customer no longer sees the option to skip or view upcoming orders?**
Customers may be confusing the Shopify order details page with the Loop Customer Portal. The Shopify order page only displays past/current orders — it does not offer Skip, Pause, or Reschedule options. Advise customers to use the "Manage Subscription" button from their Shopify order page which takes them to the actual Customer Portal.

**Sometimes a subscription order is automatically skipped by the system with no logs — why?**
This can occur when Shopify experiences downtime or temporary errors during the billing process. If Shopify is down or returns an error at the time the subscription order is attempted, Loop may mark the order as skipped. This is not a configuration issue on the merchant's side.
