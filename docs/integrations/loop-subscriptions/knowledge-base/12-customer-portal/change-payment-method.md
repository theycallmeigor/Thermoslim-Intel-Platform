# Change payment method

Learn how customers and merchants can update subscription payment methods in Loop through the customer portal, Loop admin, or Shopify admin.

## From the Loop customer portal

**Prerequisites:**
1. Customer must have a subscription on Loop
2. "Change Payment Method" must be enabled at: **Customer Portal > Preferences > Payment and Shipping Details**

**Steps:**
1. In the Customer Portal, scroll down to "Payment details"
2. Click **"Update"** — sends an email with a link to update payment details
3. Click **"Change"** — shows a list of all payment options the customer has used on Shopify/Checkout

## From the Loop admin portal

1. Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions** and search for the customer with "Subscription ID"
2. Open the subscription, scroll to "Payment details" and choose the desired option
3. **"Update"** sends an email; **"Change"** shows a list of all payment options used by the customer

## From the Shopify admin

1. Navigate to **Shopify > Customers** and look for the customer name
2. Go to the "Payment Method" tab, click on the pen icon, and choose "Replace Card"
3. Add all required information and confirm
