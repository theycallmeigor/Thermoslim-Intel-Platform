# Reschedule an upcoming order

Learn how to enable and manage order rescheduling in Loop, giving subscribers flexible delivery control while improving retention and satisfaction.

Rescheduling an upcoming order gives subscribers greater control over their delivery timing without having to skip or pause the subscription. Whether they haven't finished their previous product, are traveling, or simply need more time before their next delivery, this feature allows flexibility while helping brands retain subscribers.

## Enabling the reschedule option

1. Navigate to **Loop > Customer portal > Preferences > Order actions > Reschedule order**
2. Enable the setting to allow customers to reschedule upcoming orders directly from the customer portal. You can also define the maximum number of days by which an order can be rescheduled.

## What happens to future billing dates?

When an order is rescheduled, the next order schedule is recalculated based on the date entered by the customer/admin, using the cadence of the subscription contract.

> If an anchor day is present, the calendar will only allow rescheduling to the nearest anchor day — all other dates are greyed out. Subsequent orders are scheduled based on the new billing date.

## Rescheduling from the customer portal

1. Customer logs in, opens the desired subscription, and clicks the "Re-schedule" button in the action bar
2. The Reschedule Order drawer opens — customer selects the desired date from the calendar
   - Note: The maximum reschedule interval is restricted on the calendar (e.g., 100 days from today)
3. Click Confirm — the order is rescheduled and all subsequent orders are automatically adjusted

## Rescheduling from the admin portal

1. Navigate to **Loop > Subscriptions** and search for the customer with "Subscription ID"
2. Click the Reschedule button next to the upcoming order details
3. Select the new date from the calendar and choose required options:
   - "Reschedule subsequent subscription orders"
   - "Notify customer?"
4. Click Confirm — the order is rescheduled and all subsequent orders are updated

## FAQs

**I can only manually change a customer's subscription plan up to a certain number of months in advance. How can I adjust this limit?**
Go to: **Loop app > Customer Portal > Preferences > Order Actions > Reschedule**. Check the current reschedule interval and update it to the desired duration (in days). This allows you and customers to reschedule upcoming orders within the new set interval.
