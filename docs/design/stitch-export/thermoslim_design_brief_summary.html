# ThermoSlim Platform — UI Design Brief ## What This Is Design brief for the UI overhaul of the ThermoSlim Commerce Intelligence Platform. The platform unifies data from Shopify and CheckoutChamp (subscription CRM) into a single BI tool for a supplement e-commerce brand. Currently has 2 pages — needs to scale to 15+ dashboards. ## The Business ThermoSlim sells health/weight-loss supplements through Shopify and CheckoutChamp (CC). Subscriptions are the core business — MRR, churn, and retention are the metrics that matter most. ## Users Primary: Internal operations team (power users). Need high information density, filters everywhere, raw numbers, and ability to drill into anything. Deep links to Shopify and CC Admin are essential. ## Visual Design (Established) - Dark theme only: `bg-gray-950` root, `bg-gray-900` cards - Card pattern: `bg-gray-900 rounded-xl border border-gray-800 p-6` - Color system: - Blue `#3b82f6` — primary action, orders, links - Green `#10b981` — revenue, success, positive change, Shopify - Purple `#8b5cf6` — new subscriptions, direct sales - Orange `#f59e0b` — rebills, paused, weekend - Red `#ef4444` — cancelled, danger, negative change - Cyan `#06b6d4` — subscription orders - Typography: System font, `text-2xl font-bold` for KPI values, `text-xs uppercase tracking-wider` for labels ## Layout Rules 1. Sidebar: ~220px, dark bg (`#0f1117`), collapsible. 2. Top bar: Breadcrumb + date filter + sync trigger. 3. KPI strip: Always first, 4 cards in a row. 4. Charts: Recharts library, dark tooltip styling. 5. Slide-over panel: ~380px from right edge for details.
