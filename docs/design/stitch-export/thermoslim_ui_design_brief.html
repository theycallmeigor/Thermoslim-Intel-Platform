# ThermoSlim Platform — UI Design Brief ## What This Is Design brief for the UI overhaul of the ThermoSlim Commerce Intelligence Platform. The platform unifies data from Shopify and CheckoutChamp (subscription CRM) into a single BI tool for a supplement e-commerce brand. Currently has 2 pages — needs to scale to 15+ dashboards. ## The Business ThermoSlim sells health/weight-loss supplements through Shopify and CheckoutChamp (CC). The platform merges and deduplicates this data. Subscriptions are the core business — MRR, churn, and retention are key metrics. ## Users Internal operations team (power users). High information density, filters everywhere, raw numbers, and deep links to Shopify/CC Admin are essential. ## Current State - **Tech Stack:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts. - **Visual Design:** Dark theme (`bg-gray-950` root, `bg-gray-900` cards), `rounded-xl border border-gray-800`. - **Color System:** Blue (Primary/Orders), Green (Revenue/Shopify), Purple (New Subs), Orange (Rebills), Red (Cancelled/Danger), Cyan (Sub Orders). - **Typography:** System font, `text-2xl font-bold` for KPIs, `text-xs uppercase` for labels. ## Design Decisions 1. **Sidebar Navigation:** Persistent, collapsible, grouped by domain (Overview, Subscriptions, Orders, Performance, Operations, Settings). 2. **Order Detail:** Slide-over panel (~380px) + dedicated full-width page. 3. **Top Bar:** Breadcrumbs, last sync, date range presets, sync trigger. ## Key Screens to Design - **Upcoming Rebills:** Expected revenue by day, risk indicators, sortable tables. - **Order Detail Page (`/orders/{id}`):** Full financials, items, attribution, fulfillment, and deep links. - **Customer 360 (`/customers/{id}`):** Profile, order history, subscription timeline, and LTV summary. - **MRR Waterfall, Churn Analytics, Cohort Retention, etc.** (Tier 2). ## Layout Rules - Sidebar: ~220px, dark bg (`#0f1117`). - KPI strip: First element, 4-card grid. - Tables: Clickable rows, source/status badges. - Charts: Recharts-based, dark tooltips.
